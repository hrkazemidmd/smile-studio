Questions

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in Monday to Thursday, 1 to 5 PM. Other times may be available by prior request: call 301-654-7070.

Is there a fee to visit?

No. The Smile Studio discovery experience is complimentary, with no obligation to pursue treatment.

Do I need to know which treatment I want?

Not at all. Smile Studio is for anyone interested in learning about dental implants, whether you’re simply curious or actively considering treatment.

Will I meet Dr. Kazemi?

Smile Studio is an educational visit supported by our implant care team. A clinical consultation with Dr. Kazemi can be arranged when appropriate.

Am I expected to schedule treatment?

Absolutely not. Our goal is to help you explore, learn, and decide at your own pace.
